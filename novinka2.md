**DARIAH-CH Online Training Series 2026–2027**

**September 2026 – May 2027 | Online | Fridays | 1:30-3:00PM**  

DARIAH-CH is offering a free online training series covering various aspects of the Digital Humanities. Many of these topics are also relevant to the activities and services of EHRI-ERIC, including FAIR data, online editions, Wikidata and, of course, AI. The course aims at beginners and those who would like to improve their digital literacy.
