{{< intro >}}
A new season of the EHRI Podcast *For the Living and the Dead: Traces of the Holocaust* was launched on Wednesday 5 August 2026.
{{< /intro >}}

The fourth season of the EHRI Podcast series *For the Living and the Dead. Traces of the Holocaust* features seven new episodes around the theme **_Roma and the Holocaust_**. New podcast host Lovro Kralj – EHRI Research and Content Officer – interviews seven experts who share their insights while narrating the story of a person or object that really took their breath away.

More information: [https://www.ehri-project.eu/ehri-podcast-for-the-living-and-the-dead/](https://www.ehri-project.eu/ehri-podcast-for-the-living-and-the-dead/)
