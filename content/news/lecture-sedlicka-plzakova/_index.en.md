The Kurt and Ursula Schubert Center for Jewish Studies, Faculty of Arts, Palacký University Olomouc invites you to a lecture in the Jewish Studies Public Lecture Series: **Magdalena Sedlická and Aneta Plzáková: Holocaust in Space and Data – MemoMap and the EHRI-CZ Portal**.
{{< /intro >}}

##### Date: 21 October 2026 at 17:00

##### Venue: Library of the Center for Jewish Studies, Tř. Svobody 26, Olomouc (1st floor, room 2.36)

The lecture will focus on the European Holocaust Research Infrastructure (EHRI), emphasizing services designed for researchers and the wider public. The speakers will introduce the MemoMap application, which enables users to discover the stories of Jewish inhabitants during the Protectorate era directly in the streets of Prague, Pacov, and Říčany. Attendees will also learn about the emerging EHRI-CZ Portal – a comprehensive gateway connecting data on Holocaust victims, transports, persecution sites, and archival records from Czech and international institutions.

**Mgr. Magdalena Sedlická, Ph.D.** is the national coordinator of EHRI-CZ and a historian specializing in modern Jewish history. Among other works, she published a book on Jews in postwar Czechoslovakia.

**Mgr. Aneta Plzáková** is the data curator of EHRI-CZ, focusing on the management and integration of historical data with an emphasis on Holocaust victims' data and geospatial information.

Both speakers are affiliated with the Masaryk Institute and Archives of the Czech Academy of Sciences.

*The event is organized with the support of the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sports (MŠMT) within the CRP 202 project "Support of Strategically Important Programs at the Faculties of Arts of Charles University, Masaryk University, and Palacký University".*
