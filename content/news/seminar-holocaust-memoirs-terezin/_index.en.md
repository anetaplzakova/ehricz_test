The Vienna Wiesenthal Institute for Holocaust Studies together with the Terezín Memorial are organizing an EHRI-ERIC transnational seminar on Holocaust ego-documents and memoirs from 30 November to 3 December 2026, including a public discussion with Alica Frühwaldová.
{{< /intro >}}

##### Seminar Date: 30 November – 3 December 2026

##### Venue: Terezín Memorial, Principova alej 304, Terezín

The Vienna Wiesenthal Institute for Holocaust Studies, together with the Terezín Memorial (a partner institution of EHRI-CZ), is holding a transnational seminar from 30 November to 3 December 2026, initiated by an EHRI-ERIC working group focusing on Holocaust ego-documents. The seminar builds upon existing research on memoir literature.

### Public Discussion

EHRI-CZ cordially invites you to a public discussion taking place on **30 November from 6:00 PM to 7:30 PM** at the Terezín Memorial.

**Monika Vrzgulová** (Slovak Academy of Sciences) will be in conversation with **Alica Frühwaldová** about her book ***The Loves I Have Lost (O koľko lásky som prišla)***.

For more information about the seminar, visit the [EHRI website](https://www.ehri-project.eu/call-for-applications-ehri-eric-transnational-seminar-holocaust-memoirs-who-will-write-their-history/).
